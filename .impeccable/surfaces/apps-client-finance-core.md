## Grounded direction order and the hand

Seven derivations were ordered before implementation: city public wayfinding;
museum collection labels; park wayfinding; contemporary album typography;
parcel tracking labels; library classification; contemporary atlas precision.
They span environmental identity, cultural artefacts and data graphics.

Atlas precision takes only palette, typography, density and one signature move;
it never replaces native navigation or controls with a map.

The public-wayfinding pick remained a full alternative, with its familiarity
stated. Boarding-pass/gate-board was competitive on task clarity. Vertical-video
feed, orienteering map, centre-rail reference setting, Crouwel grid and Datamatics
were declined for their specific product-clarity or identification failures.
The authored full hand and reasons are in
`.impeccable/mocks/decision-finance-core/question.json`; it is a design decision,
not a claim that HTML proves native UI.

Named raises: boarding-board row identity and persistent change acknowledgment;
reference-setting baselines and disciplined amounts; grid-specimen density
discipline. No decorative boarding passes, map contours, grids or strobing.

## Quality bar

- A normal phone first viewport exposes meaningful activity and the next action;
  large headers, pastel action walls and repeated 80px rows are the anti-reference.
- All core tasks have an entry, valid input, visible commit result, return path,
  error/retry, and correction/undo where the owning model supports it.
- Cash, quantity, cost, price, income, gains and net worth remain distinct. Missing
  evidence is unavailable, never fabricated history, a zero price or FX=1.
- Use native route depth and short, well-damped selection/feedback transitions.
  The signature is an action's affected fact acknowledged in place. Reduced motion
  shows the final state immediately. Never count money through floating point.
- Shared forms preserve dirty drafts, prevent duplicate commits, support real date
  selection, and keep actions above the keyboard and system navigation.
- Keep explicit input text/hint/caret roles, Handled scroll taps, and modal-window
  keyboard dismissal. Chart semantics require real native text and data rows.
- Validate complete synthetic Finance journeys on Android, including repeated
  navigation/forms, failure recovery, restart, new-family backup, dark appearance,
  narrow widths and enlarged text. A build or headless test alone is insufficient.

Catalog quality-bar URLs are retained in the decision payload. Direct reference
image downloads failed with HTTP errors in this environment; no missing image is
represented as viewed or as a delivered visual asset.

## Core acceptance boundaries

Connect accounts and opening balances; assets and valuations; loan schedules and
payments; income/expense/transfers; reviewed CSV import and duplicate evidence;
category budgets; recurring obligations and cash runway; holdings, FIFO trades,
fees, dividends and splits; portfolios, exclusive capital and rebalance receipts;
goals, DCA and FIRE assumptions; Inbox, reconciliation and five-step monthly close.
All use the same owner-scoped ledger and exact monetary facts. Other LifeOS domains
and Flutter remain outside this implementation. No broker execution, cloud AI
relay, fabricated model review, old Flutter migration, options or paper simulation.
